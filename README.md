# [Best-Low-Latency-Wireless-Headsets-for-Console](https://benchmarksetups.com/low-latency-wireless-headset-console-tested/)
Best Low-Latency Wireless Headsets for Console: Audio Delay Tested
You hear a footstep, and then the other player is already around the corner. Or it goes the other way: you see him, you shoot, and your headset is still catching up. If that sounds familiar, the headset may not be the problem. How it talks to your console often matters more.

I ran three popular console headsets through the free Bluetooth Lag Meter that lives on this page. Below you will find what I measured, what those numbers can and cannot tell you, and what I would check before buying a wireless headset for a PS5 or Xbox.

Consoles do not do Bluetooth headset audio
This catches a lot of people out. On a phone or a PC, any Bluetooth headphones just work. On a PS5 or an Xbox Series X|S, they do not. Neither console supports standard Bluetooth audio for headsets.

So wireless headsets for console come with their own USB dongle or base station, usually on a 2.4 GHz link. The other options are a cable into the controller's 3.5 mm jack, or a third-party Bluetooth adapter. Those adapters exist, but they add a step and, as a rule, more delay.
